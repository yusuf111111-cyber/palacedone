MCC Road Construction Completed by His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado

August 12, 2025

His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado oversaw the construction of MCC Road in Okpella, improving transportation and connectivity for the community.

The new road reduces travel times, supports local trade and improves access to markets and services for residents across the region.

The Palace described the road as part of broader infrastructure priorities to support economic growth and regional development.
