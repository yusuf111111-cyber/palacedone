His Royal Majesty Receives Distinguished Guests At The Royal Palace

July 20, 2026 4 min read

His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado graciously welcomed a delegation of distinguished guests to the Royal Palace, reaffirming the Palace's commitment to peace, unity, cultural preservation and sustainable development through meaningful partnerships and dialogue.

The delegation included leaders from regional community organisations, cultural custodians and development partners. Discussions focused on collaborative projects to support education, healthcare, and local enterprise.

The Royal Palace emphasised the importance of inclusive planning and community-driven initiatives, and pledged to work closely with partners to translate agreements into tangible benefits for residents across the kingdom.
