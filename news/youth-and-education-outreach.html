Palace Expands Youth and Education Outreach Programme

August 1, 2026

New outreach efforts are connecting young people and families with more mentoring, learning support and cultural engagement opportunities.

The programme includes school partnerships, mentorship schemes and community learning events designed to broaden opportunities for youth and strengthen educational outcomes across the kingdom.

The Palace encourages parents and community organisations to get involved to ensure programmes meet local needs and reach those who will benefit most.
