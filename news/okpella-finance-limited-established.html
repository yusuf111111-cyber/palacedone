His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado Establishes Okpella Finance Limited

September 30, 2025

His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado has established Okpella Finance Limited, a financial institution aimed at providing accessible banking services and promoting economic opportunities for local businesses and residents.

The institution will offer tailored financial products and support for small and medium enterprises, with the goal of fostering economic inclusion and sustainable economic growth in the kingdom.
