Annual Okpella Cultural Festival Celebrated

July 12, 2026

The Royal Palace hosted the Annual Cultural Festival, bringing together performers, artisans and community groups to celebrate the traditions and customs of Okpella.

Attendees enjoyed traditional dance and music, cultural exhibitions, and showcases of local crafts and cuisine. The festival is part of the Palace's effort to preserve cultural heritage and encourage community participation.

Organisers emphasised opportunities for youth engagement and cultural education to ensure traditions are passed to future generations.
