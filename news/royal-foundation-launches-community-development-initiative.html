Royal Foundation Launches Community Development Initiative

June 30, 2026 5 min read

The Royal Foundation introduced a community development programme focusing on empowerment, education and sustainable growth.

Through targeted grants, skills training and partnerships with local organisations, the programme seeks to create opportunities for young people and small businesses across Okpella Kingdom.

Initial projects include vocational training workshops, educational scholarships and support for community health outreach.
