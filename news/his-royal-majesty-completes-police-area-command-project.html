His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado Completes Okpella Police Area Command Station Project

March 30, 2026

His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado has successfully completed the Okpella Police Area Command Station project, enhancing security and community safety in the region.

The completed facility will improve police response capabilities and provide a central point for community policing initiatives and collaboration with local stakeholders.

The Palace highlighted the project's role in creating a safer environment for commerce, education and daily life across the kingdom.
