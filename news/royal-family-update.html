Royal Family Announces New Community Outreach Initiative

August 10, 2026

The Royal Family has launched a new community outreach initiative focusing on youth mentorship, cultural education and support for local artisans. The programme is designed to strengthen ties between the Palace and communities across Okpella Kingdom.

Activities will include skills workshops, scholarship opportunities and cultural exchange events intended to preserve traditional arts while creating economic opportunities for young people.

The Royal Family invites community leaders and residents to participate and share ideas to ensure the initiative meets local needs and supports sustainable development.
