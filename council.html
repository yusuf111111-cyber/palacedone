The Royal Council

Preserving Tradition, Guiding The Kingdom

The Royal Council of His Royal Majesty, Michael E.O. Sado, the Okuokpellagbe of Okpella Kingdom, stands as a distinguished institution dedicated to preserving the rich cultural heritage, customs and traditions of the Okpella people while promoting peace, unity and sustainable development throughout the kingdom.

Serving as the principal advisory body to His Royal Majesty, the Royal Council comprises respected chiefs, elders, title holders and community leaders whose collective wisdom and experience contribute significantly to the administration and progress of Okpella Kingdom.

Guided by the enduring values of integrity, justice, service and respect for tradition, the Council works closely with the throne to uphold the dignity, stability and unity of the kingdom.

Roles & Responsibilities

Dedicated To Service, Unity & Good Governance

The Royal Council serves as a trusted advisory body, working alongside His Royal Majesty to preserve the customs of the kingdom while promoting peace, justice, responsible leadership and sustainable development.

Justice

Encouraging fairness, dialogue and peaceful conflict resolution across communities.

Unity

Strengthening relationships between communities, leaders and the traditional institution.

Cultural Preservation

Protecting the customs, traditions and identity that define Okpella Kingdom.

Traditional Leadership

Supporting His Royal Majesty through wisdom, experience and responsible governance.

The Royal Council plays a vital role in fostering harmony among communities, encouraging dialogue, promoting conflict resolution and strengthening the bond between the traditional institution and the people. Through its leadership, the Council continues to safeguard the customs and cultural identity that have defined Okpella Kingdom for generations, ensuring that these treasured traditions remain vibrant for future generations.

Community Development

Building Stronger Communities Through Partnership & Progress

Beyond its traditional responsibilities, the Royal Council remains committed to supporting initiatives that advance education, youth empowerment, healthcare, infrastructure, environmental sustainability and economic development throughout Okpella Kingdom.

The Council actively encourages collaboration with government agencies, private organisations, educational institutions and development partners to improve the quality of life and create lasting opportunities for the people of the kingdom.

Through strategic partnerships and responsible leadership, the Royal Council continues to promote programmes that strengthen communities, encourage innovation and contribute meaningfully to the long-term prosperity of Okpella Kingdom.

Leadership & Governance

Honouring Tradition, Embracing Progress

Under the visionary leadership of His Royal Majesty Michael E.O. Sado, the Royal Council has embraced a progressive approach that combines deep respect for ancestral values with a strong commitment to modern development and community advancement.

This balanced approach has strengthened the role of the traditional institution as an important partner in promoting social stability, responsible governance and inclusive development throughout Okpella Kingdom.

Through collaboration, wisdom and accountable leadership, the Royal Council continues to support initiatives that improve the lives of the people while ensuring that the customs and traditions of the kingdom remain respected and preserved.

Cultural Heritage

Preserving The Traditions That Define Our Kingdom

The Royal Council serves as a custodian of the rich history and cultural legacy of Okpella Kingdom, supporting traditional festivals, ceremonies and cultural events that celebrate the identity, achievements and enduring values of the Okpella people.

Through these cultural activities, the Council promotes unity, strengthens cultural pride and inspires younger generations to appreciate, preserve and proudly carry forward the traditions passed down by their ancestors.

By safeguarding the customs and heritage of the kingdom, the Royal Council ensures that the spirit of Okpella continues to flourish while remaining relevant to future generations.

Our Mission

United By Service, Guided By Responsibility

Every member of the Royal Council shares a common commitment to preserving the dignity of the throne, serving the people and advancing the future of Okpella Kingdom through responsible traditional leadership.

Leadership

Providing wise counsel and supporting His Royal Majesty in the administration of the kingdom.

Supporting initiatives that improve lives and create opportunities for future generations.

Members of the Royal Council are united by a shared commitment to selfless service, accountability and the advancement of the kingdom. Their dedication reflects the enduring ideals of leadership, wisdom and responsibility that have long characterised the traditional institution of the Okuokpellagbe.

As the kingdom continues to grow and evolve, the Royal Council remains steadfast in its mission to support His Royal Majesty in building a peaceful, prosperous and united Okpella Kingdom. Through its collective efforts, the Council continues to strengthen the foundations of good traditional governance, preserve the dignity of the throne and contribute meaningfully to the social and economic progress of the kingdom.
