About OFL

Building Financial Opportunities For Every Community

Okpella Finance Limited (OFL) is a forward-looking, non-bank financial institution established to promote financial inclusion, support enterprise development and expand access to modern financial services throughout Okpella Kingdom and beyond.

Built upon a vision of responsible finance and community empowerment, the institution creates opportunities that enable individuals, families and businesses to thrive within an increasingly digital economy through innovative financial solutions and strategic partnerships.

Inspired by the development vision of Okpella Kingdom, Okpella Finance Limited remains committed to bridging the gap between underserved communities and formal financial services while encouraging entrepreneurship, local commerce and long-term economic growth.
